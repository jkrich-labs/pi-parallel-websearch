# content_research_brief

**Goal:** Research brief for an article/post on topic T.

**Cost estimate:** ~$0.03, ~5 min.

## Steps

### 1. Establish the landscape
- tool: `web_search` — objective: "What are the main positions, debates, and recent developments in <topic>?"; search_queries: ["<topic> 2026", "<topic> state of the art", "<topic> debate"]; mode: fast

### 2. Deepen the top sources
- tool: `web_fetch` — urls: [top 5-8 URLs]; objective: "Extract key claims, numbers, named experts, and quotes"

### 3. Verify discrepancies
- tool: `web_answer` — question: "Which claims about <topic> conflict between <source A> and <source B>?"; reasoning_effort: low
- why: a brief that names conflicts writes better than one that averages them.

**Deliverable:** brief with: landscape summary, key stats (cited), expert names (cited), open questions.
