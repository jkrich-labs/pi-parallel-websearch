# meeting_prep

**Goal:** Prep brief for a meeting with <company/contact>.

**Cost estimate:** ~$0.02, ~4 min.

## Steps

### 1. Search the company
- tool: `web_search` — objective: "Recent news, product moves, and public statements by <company> relevant to a meeting with <contact>"; search_queries: ["<company> news 2026", "<contact> <company>"]; mode: fast

### 2. Search the person
- tool: `web_search` — objective: "Who is <contact> at <company>: role, background, recent public appearances"; search_queries: ["<contact> <company>"], mode: fast

### 3. Verify from primary sources
- tool: `web_fetch` — urls: [company press page, person's LinkedIn/profile]; objective: "Extract role, focus areas, recent quotes"

**Deliverable:** 6-bullet brief (company state, contact context, 2-3 likely topics, open questions).
